Robert's care today

Reviewed and shared by Dr. Morgan, today at 08:15.

What's happening

Robert's heart is having trouble keeping up with what his body needs. Because of that, fluid has built up in his lungs and legs, and that is why he has been short of breath. Doctors call this heart failure. It does not mean the heart has stopped. It means the heart needs help to keep up.

He is breathing more easily than when he arrived, and his weight is going down as the extra fluid leaves his body.

What the care team is doing

  • Water pills through his IV to remove the extra fluid.
  • A heart ultrasound (echocardiogram) to see how well his heart pumps and relaxes.
  • Daily blood tests to check his kidneys and potassium, a mineral that must stay in a safe range.
  • Weighing him every morning to see how much fluid has come off.

How you can help

  • Bring a list (or the bottles) of every medicine and supplement he takes at home.
  • Tell the nurse if he uses a salt substitute. Many contain potassium.
  • Please don't bring salty snacks for now.

Questions you might ask the team

"What did the heart ultrasound show?"
"Which medicines will change when he goes home?"
"What weight gain should make us call the doctor?"
