NNOMI Care

The call light, reinvented.

Every request goes to the right person in seconds. The patient sees who's coming and when. The nurse gets the context they need before walking in, and the notes are drafted for them. Nurses get their time back for nursing.

Try it: tap Pain on the patient phone, then On my way on the care team phone.
Encrypted · real-time

Design prototype with a fictional patient. NOMI Care adds to the nurse call system and to nursing judgment. It never replaces them: anything clinical is routed to a registered nurse, and the bed's red call button always works. Routing rules, fluid limits and PRN intervals come from the patient's actual orders and unit policy.
