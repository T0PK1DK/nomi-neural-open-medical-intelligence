    Where NOMI's knowledge comes from

    NOMI is built on open, public sources first. Copyrighted guidelines are summarized and linked, never republished. Every reference shows how much of it you can read for free.

    The NOMI evidence policy

    1. No sponsored content in clinical answers. No ads in answers or while they load, and no paid placement in source ranking.
    2. Every claim is cited to a source you can open. If NOMI can't cite it, NOMI doesn't say it.
    3. Guidelines before single studies, and studies before opinion. Recommendation class and level of evidence are quoted from the guideline itself.
    4. Patient context is opt-in, visible and auditable. The context chip shows exactly what NOMI read.
    5. One evidence base, three reading levels. Clinician, nurse and patient versions cite the same sources.
    6. Unknowns are stated, not filled in.

    NOMI Atlas is a design prototype. The patient is fictional, and the cited publications are real. Answers are summaries for professional review and do not replace clinical judgment or the original sources.
